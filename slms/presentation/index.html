07 · TRUST BY DESIGN

Simple on the surface.
Serious underneath.

01

Role boundaries

Student and administrator portals run independently, with wrong-area routes redirected.

02

Protected sessions

Sessions are stored in MongoDB and expire after eight hours of inactivity.

03

Safer credentials

Passwords are hashed with bcryptjs and forms use CSRF protection.

04

Clean records

Validation, unique indexes, file limits, and timestamps keep data usable.
